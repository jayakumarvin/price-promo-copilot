# Promotion Rules

Sample policy text written for this demo. Replace it with your real policy documents.

## Depth and duration
- The minimum discount that counts as a promotion is 10% off the list price.
- The maximum discount for a standard temporary price reduction (TPR) is 30%.
- A promotion runs for 1 to 4 consecutive weeks.
- After a promotion ends, the same SKU in the same market has a cool-down of 4 weeks before the next promotion.

## Frequency
- No SKU may be promoted for more than 13 weeks in a rolling 52-week period.
- Promoting more than 25% of a category's SKUs in the same week needs Category Manager approval.

## Evaluation
- Judge every promotion on incremental margin, not on units alone.
- Compare promoted weeks with a baseline built from non-promoted weeks.
- Flag a promotion for review when its trade spend is larger than its incremental gross margin.
