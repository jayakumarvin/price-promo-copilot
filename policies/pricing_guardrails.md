# Pricing Guardrails

Sample policy text written for this demo. Replace it with your real policy documents.

## Price change limits
- A permanent list price change must stay within 10% of the current list price in any 4-week period.
- A change of more than 10% needs sign-off from the Category Manager.
- Prices must never be set below unit cost plus the minimum margin floor (see Margin Floors).

## Price ending rules
- Consumer prices end in .49, .95 or .99. Do not use other endings without approval.
- Related pack sizes of one product must keep a consistent price per unit. The larger pack must not cost more per unit than the smaller one.

## Elasticity evidence
- A price increase proposal must cite an elasticity estimate that is negative and statistically reliable.
- If the estimated elasticity is between -1 and 0 (inelastic), a small price increase is expected to raise revenue. Check the margin effect as well.
- If the estimate is positive or not significant, treat it as unreliable and do not use it as the only reason for a price change.
