# Margin Floors and Trade Spend

Sample policy text written for this demo. Replace it with your real policy documents.

## Margin floors
- Every SKU must keep a gross margin of at least 20% of net price, also during a promotion.
- A promotion that takes a SKU below the 20% floor needs Finance approval.
- Gross margin is net price minus unit cost, divided by net price.

## Trade spend
- Trade spend is the discount given per unit multiplied by the units sold on promotion.
- The trade spend budget for a market is capped at 8% of its planned revenue for the quarter.
- Spend above 80% of the budget before the last month of the quarter triggers a review.

## Reporting
- Report margin and trade spend together. A promotion with high lift but negative incremental margin is a failure.
- State which costs and promotion dates are measured and which are assumed or inferred.
